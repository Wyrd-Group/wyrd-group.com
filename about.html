In one sentence

Wyrd Group builds governed, local-first and cloud-optional AI systems that let people, institutions and machines use intelligence without surrendering authority, privacy, resilience or proof.

Not one chatbot, and not a loose collection of unrelated products. Wyrd is a reusable software platform whose components are assembled differently for each domain that has to carry consequence.

The name

Wyrd means fate — events woven, not isolated.

It also sounds like "weird," which is the method: look at a familiar problem from an unfamiliar angle, preserve questions the industry stopped asking, translate unusual ideas into engineering primitives, then challenge them with experts, simulations, tests, law, ethics and proof. Keep the survivors. Discard the rest.

Memorable names above. Precise claims underneath.

The relationship

Wyrd Labs creates the software. Wyrd divisions turn it into market products. The divisions package the shared stack, adapt it to a market, obtain design partners, build the domain workflow, validate it, sell it, deploy it and support it.

Governance

Every quarter, Wyrd judges AI — then judges itself.

Three standing functions force power to face judgment before leadership decides, and put that judgment on a public schedule.

Advisory

High Advisory Council of Judgment and Restraint

  • Executives and board members
  • Religious thinkers, philosophers, humanists
  • Scientists, safety and security engineers
  • Lawyers, economists, policy figures
  • Military professionals and sector experts
  • Affected communities
Adversarial

The Accuser's Office

  • Find hidden assumptions
  • Propose failure cases
  • Challenge market logic and ethics
  • Challenge safety and feasibility
  • Challenge the evidence itself
  • Recommend kill, repair or retest
Public

Quarterly State of AI and Restraint

  • Concerns about the wider AI industry
  • Per-system self-assessments
  • Minority views, recorded
  • Evidence and unresolved risks
  • Changes since the prior quarter
  • Verdict scale: green → black
Two boundaries on the council

It is advisory — not a product-launch veto body. And it is deliberately plural: the intended perspectives span Judaism, Christianity, Islam, Buddhism, Hindu traditions, secular ethics, humanism, philosophy, science, human rights, democratic legitimacy, international humanitarian law, economics, security and sector practice. It is not designed to ask one ideology to bless a decision.

Solomon may listen, transcribe, identify agreements and disagreements and compile a draft conclusion with its own synthesis separately labelled. The final meeting confirms the publication says what the humans concluded — not what Solomon guessed they meant.

Wide, cheap and ruthless.

Externally

Narrow and provable.

  • The wider map is optionality, not a sales promise
  • Every claim tied to evidence that exists
Roadmap

Five horizons, in order.

H0
Now. Consolidate canon, pin releases, finish the public proof register, make the product hierarchy legible, select first design partners, demonstrate governed local-plus-cloud enterprise AI.
H1
Controlled pilots. Enterprise and bank agents, Themis clubs, a clinician-governed Raphael study, TrueSight authentication, ARGOS sector dashboards, NINA benchmarked on real constraints, HIVE in simulation, RADOME continuity, CTOS read-only, Anchorline, Wyrd ARK monitoring.
H2
Productization. Signed installers, reproducible deployments, RASOS end-to-end, independent security review, privacy and legal packs, partner case studies, hardware adapters, regulatory pathways where needed.
H3
Hardware and fleet. Trust-surface devices, certified drones and robots, Leviathan partner vehicles, Phaeton bodies, Wyrd Lens, Biota wearables, ORION field pods, persistent deployments.
H4
Ecosystem. A T-AOS device family, an app and skill marketplace, certified partner hardware, and large-scale networks — plus any economic layer only if it is legally and commercially justified.
Language

The sentences we refuse to write.

Copy discipline is part of the product. If a claim cannot be checked, it does not go on this site.

Never written here
  • "Wyrd has built every product shown"
  • "Wyrd is fully certified"
  • "Wyrd replaces doctors"
  • "Local AI is free" · "Wyrd eliminates data centers"
  • "Wyrd AI is perfectly private" · "perfect accuracy" · "perfect safety"
  • "Wyrd controls cities" · "Wyrd operates autonomous weapons"
  • "Wyrd can predict geopolitics" · "Wyrd has solved optimization"
  • "Wyrd's drones are field proven" · "Wyrd owns satellites" · "Wyrd manufactures submarines"
  • "Wyrd is a bank, payment rail, exchange or currency issuer"
  • "Tests prove production readiness"

Instead: designed to · working alpha · pilot candidate · research program · partner hardware · current evidence · subject to validation · human authority · proof-backed · local-first and cloud-optional.

Naming discipline

Some names in our history collide. The current canon: IRIS is the Ingestion, Recall & Integrity System (the old geopolitics module of that name is retired). NINA supersedes NCOE. Samael (civilian protection) is never merged with Azrael. Phaeton is robotics, Leviathan is maritime, and the current Anchorline is property and factory security, not the parked maritime direction. Personal consumer systems belong to Biota, not to "Nestar Personal."
