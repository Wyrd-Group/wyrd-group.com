ABOUT WYRD

Intelligence that can work.
People who remain in control.

Wyrd Group is building a modular intelligence and operations ecosystem. Our ambition is to help people and organisations use capable AI while retaining control of confidential information, delegated authority and verifiable outcomes.

Unification and ontology are central to that ambition: different applications, data sources and machines should share an understanding of the same entities, relationships and events. Useful intelligence can then follow work from a question to an observed result.
