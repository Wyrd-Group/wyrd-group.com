WHY WE EXIST

An idea deserves
a way into the world.

A person can recognise a problem long before they know the technical language used to describe its solution. Wyrd exists to help close the distance between that understanding and the ability to make something useful. AI can help someone learn, investigate, construct, test and improve. Their experience and intention give the work its destination.

We develop intelligence and operations technology for people, institutions and physical systems. Our ambition is to carry understanding from a question through permitted work to an observed consequence, while keeping the people affected in control of the powers they delegate.

THE FIRST PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Alec de Carvalho, Founder, CEO & Chief Systems Architect

Questions first.
Then the work of making.

Alec founded Wyrd to make the distance between an idea and something useful smaller. His work began with questions, metaphors and connections between unfamiliar fields, using AI collaborators to acquire knowledge, develop software and test what those ideas could become. That experience continues to shape how he approaches the company: begin with the problem, understand the relationships beneath it, and build something whose usefulness can be examined.

As Chief Systems Architect, Alec defines how Wyrd’s intelligence, products and physical-system programmes fit together. He determines what belongs in the shared foundations, what each component is responsible for, how information moves between systems, and where decisions require human authority. He translates the founding vision into architectural principles, product requirements and priorities, working with the team to challenge assumptions, develop the implementation and establish what the resulting capability can reliably do.

Wyrd Unified Intelligence gives this work a common direction. Its architectural ambition connects knowledge and memory with perception, reasoning, coordination and action. A question should retain its context as different models and tools contribute. A decision should remain connected to its purpose and authority. An action should return an observed result that can be compared with what was expected, preserving the experience needed to improve the next decision.

Across the ecosystem, Alec connects the shared research and engineering of Wyrd Labs and the design and interaction work of Wyrd Creative with WyrdWork, ARGOS, Biota and Corpus. He looks for foundations that can serve several products while preserving each product’s purpose, each customer’s information boundaries and the qualification required for its operating environment.

“One intelligence. Many modes.” expresses the conviction behind that responsibility. Different disciplines offer different perspectives on the same world. His role is to make those perspectives work together through understandable relationships, clear responsibilities and evidence of what happens.

As CEO, Alec also leads company strategy, product direction, partnerships and team development, connecting the architecture to customer needs and sustainable delivery. The purpose running through both roles is to expand what people and institutions can understand, create and accomplish, while keeping the powers they delegate under their control.

How Labs turns an idea into a tested capability

The founding team

Co-founder · Engineering & Research

Rafael Reis

Rafael brings technical invention, engineering judgement and research to Wyrd’s founding team. His work begins with the problem itself: understanding the constraints, questioning the existing approach and exploring whether a different mechanism could make something useful possible.

Through Wyrd Labs, he helps shape the company’s technological direction alongside Alec. His contribution includes finding problems, originating new systems and examining how an idea could be built. That means considering its mechanisms, dependencies, resource requirements and development sequence, then deciding what needs to be investigated or tested before the team can rely on it.

Across the ecosystem, this work connects research to the foundations that WyrdWork, ARGOS, Biota and Corpus can build upon. A useful discovery should become understandable, maintainable technology, with clear interfaces and evidence of its behaviour. Each product and operating environment then brings its own integration and qualification requirements.

Rafael and Alec develop the relationship between the wider architecture and the technical possibilities within it. Alec’s responsibility for coherence across the company meets Rafael’s scrutiny of mechanisms, feasibility and implementation. Research can change the architecture; architectural questions can give research a new direction. AI expands what the team can investigate and construct, while people remain responsible for examining what it produces.

Working with Maxime and Cal, Rafael also helps connect engineering decisions to institutional needs, product usefulness and delivery commitments. His underlying ambition is technology that leaves people with greater capability of their own: the knowledge and means to understand, maintain and improve the systems they use.

Co-founder · Strategic Relationships

Maxime Binard

Maxime connects Wyrd’s capabilities with the people and institutions through which they can become useful. His work brings relationship development, institutional understanding and practical follow-through into the founding team.

A promising conversation needs a path towards a defined undertaking. Maxime helps establish what an organisation needs, who should participate, what each party can contribute and which responsibilities must be agreed. Resources, access, expectations and the evidence required to move forward all belong within that understanding. A relationship should become stronger as the work becomes more concrete.

Across Wyrd, this perspective helps connect shared technology to different institutional circumstances. The same underlying capability may serve several environments, but each organisation has its own priorities, constraints and ways of working. Maxime helps bring those differences into the discussion early enough to shape the project itself.

His contribution complements Alec’s architectural direction and Rafael’s technological judgement. Understanding how the systems fit together is accompanied by understanding how people and organisations can work together around them. Alongside Cal, he helps connect strategic relationships, product opportunities and commercial judgement to a scope the team can pursue responsibly.

His management studies at ESCP and experience in business operations contribute to this attention to organisations and the people within them. At Wyrd, the purpose is to develop cooperation that produces lasting capability, with clear commitments and a practical understanding of what each participant needs to succeed.

Co-founder · Products & Business Development

Cal Poppink

Cal brings entrepreneurship, product-building experience and business development to Wyrd’s founding team. His work connects what the company can create with the people who need it, the reasons they would adopt it and the conditions under which it can remain useful.

His background includes published mobile applications and work with Falas, an AI interview-practice platform. This gives him experience with the relationship between a product idea, its implementation and the experience of the person using it. Moving from a capability to a useful product requires attention to how it is explained, accessed, supported and improved. His published applications are listed under Cal Poppink’s Apple developer account.

Within Wyrd, his business-development responsibility centres on understanding customer problems, qualifying opportunities, developing commercial relationships and helping turn discussions into clearly scoped work. He brings those conversations back into product and delivery decisions, so the company’s direction can respond to what people actually need.

Cal complements Alec’s work on the coherence of the ecosystem and Rafael’s development of its technical foundations with a perspective grounded in product adoption and commercial usefulness. Working alongside Maxime, he helps connect relationships and institutional opportunities to a proposition people can understand and a commitment Wyrd can deliver.

That contribution extends across the ecosystem. Institutional software, personal intelligence and physical-system programmes have different users, purchasing decisions and support requirements. Cal helps keep those differences visible as the shared technology develops, connecting ambition to customer value and sustainable commercial activity.

Different perspectives. Shared responsibility.

Wyrd’s founding team connects company direction, systems architecture, technical invention, institutional relationships and commercial development. Alec works on the coherence of the whole. Rafael develops its technological possibilities and engineering foundations. Maxime builds the relationships and agreements through which cooperation becomes practical. These responsibilities inform one another. A customer’s problem can expose a missing capability. An engineering discovery can change a product’s direction. A new relationship can create an opportunity for research. Each person contributes ideas and judgement beyond a single function. The work becomes stronger when those perspectives remain connected to the same purpose, commitments and observed results.

OUR FOUNDING CONVICTION

Reality remains whole.
Understanding follows the connections.

Monism is Wyrd's founding conviction: beneath the divisions of our knowledge, reality remains whole. We approach the same event through different perspectives. Intelligence becomes more useful when it can connect those perspectives without losing the situation, purpose and responsibility that give them meaning.

For WUI, that unity is an architectural ambition expressed through continuity: relevant permitted memory, a common objective, a shared account of the situation, and a history connecting decisions, actions and results. Different deployments retain their own identities, information boundaries and authority.
