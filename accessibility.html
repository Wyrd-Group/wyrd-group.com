Accessibility

The reveal is decoration. The content never is.

This site uses a decode animation as its signature. It is built so that assistive technology, reduced-motion users and keyboard users get the full content without the theatre.

01
Reduced motion honored. With prefers-reduced-motion set, decode effects, typing terminals and scroll scenes render as static, complete content.
02
Real text behind the animation. While a heading visually decodes, its true text is exposed to screen readers; the cycling glyphs are hidden from assistive tech.
03
Keyboard. Menus, tabs (arrow keys, Home, End), expandable rows and forms are operable without a mouse; focus is managed in the mobile drawer and returns where you left.
04
Contrast. Body and label colors are tuned to meet 4.5:1 against the near-black background, including the small mono labels.
05
Known limitations. The canvas point-field scenes are decorative and unlabeled by design; some SVG figures summarize their content in a single alt description rather than element-by-element. PDFs offered during diligence may vary.
06
