ACCESSIBILITY

Access to the content comes first.

Our target is WCAG 2.2 AA. This is a target, not a claim of independently verified conformance.

Reduced motion. The site honours the browser's reduced-motion preference. Background video can be paused, and the headline remains readable without its decode animation.

Readable information. Product descriptions are real HTML text. Decorative video is hidden from assistive technology, while informational photographs have text descriptions.

Navigation. Product names link to separate pages. The mobile menu uses a button with an expanded-state label.

Known limits. A complete keyboard, screen-reader and contrast audit of this new edition remains outstanding. Browser video playback and layout have not been independently verified.

Report a barrier. Contact alec.carvalho@wyrd-group.com with the page address and steps needed to reproduce it.
