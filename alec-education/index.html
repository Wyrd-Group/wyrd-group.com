Purpose, scope and availability+

ALEC Education combines tutoring, a governed research environment, project work and a durable learning record. Its ambition is an apprenticeship experience that helps learners explain, build, test and demonstrate useful capability across languages and contexts.

Programme delivery requires safeguarding, age-appropriate design, educator support and independent outcomes. The software is not presented as an accredited school platform.
