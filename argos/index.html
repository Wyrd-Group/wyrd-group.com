THE ARGOS DEPARTMENT AND COMMERCIAL OFFER

Intelligence. Surveillance.
Governed operations.

ARGOS is Wyrd’s intelligence department and a customer-facing commercial division. Its programme covers data analytics, intelligence, spyware and investigative software, surveillance, defence intelligence, drones, unmanned vehicles and autonomous-system control.

Across Wyrd, ARGOS develops shared perception, ontology, evidence methods and operational context. For customers, it brings selected capabilities together into a defined programme, with its own delivery, integration and support responsibilities.
