# Wyrd public film: production credits

Narration: David, Deep and Engaging Storyteller, generated with the ElevenLabs plugin. The international map passage has a new take including Dubai.

Music: the original approved V7 ElevenLabs score, arranged around curiosity, understanding, agency and expansion. Warm strings, rounded bass, restrained electronics and organic percussion. The final cue follows the revised narration and resolves at the ending.

Visuals: original Wyrd editorial animation, with native wide and portrait compositions. Archival product surfaces retain their dates and alpha status.

Berlin map: real street geometry with simulated mission overlays. The user-selected origins are Sophie-Charlotten-Straße 117 and ESCP Berlin in Charlottenburg. The destinations are The Social Hub, Alexanderstraße 40, and Alexanderplatz. The names form a small Easter egg about Wyrd's beginnings. Public-road vehicle endpoints are snapped to the connected road graph, rather than asserting driving access inside buildings or a pedestrian square. All tasks are illustrative, not claims about these organisations or operational deployments.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. [OpenStreetMap copyright and licence](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The map is rendered locally from vector data; OSM raster tiles were not used in the film.

Location verification: [ESCP official campus address](https://extension.escp.eu/contact) and [The Social Hub official Berlin address](https://support.thesocialhub.co/hc/s/articles/Addressdirectionsparkingcontactinformation67bc3cd68c8a8?language=en_US). Sophie-Charlotten-Straße 117 uses the actual OSM address node 7532936710. Geographic sources and route checks are retained in the editable project.

International map: genuine Natural Earth country geometry, public domain. The network, including Dubai as the Middle East platform, is an intended 2028 structure. The 2029 and 2030 African expansion phases remain distinct. City pins are approximate centres, not office addresses.

AI-assisted production. Synthetic narration. Physical programmes and workflows retain their on-screen qualification labels.
