Purpose, scope and availability+

The planned assurance programme would test a defined integration against its authority, disclosure and execution requirements. It would examine denial paths, evidence coverage, key custody, unexpected outcomes and recovery. Technical results support customer review; they do not constitute automatic regulatory certification.

The delivery scope, team, dates, support and acceptance requirements are agreed for each project. This page describes the intended service offer, not a claim of a completed customer deployment.
