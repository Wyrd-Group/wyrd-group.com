THE INDIVIDUAL'S CONTEXT

Assistance that understands
who it is there to serve.

Biota develops the personal side of Wyrd's shared foundations. Its ambition is assistance that can connect relevant knowledge, context and memory around the person, while keeping access and delegated authority understandable.

Personal and institutional information have different owners, purposes and boundaries. A shared architecture does not make a person's private memory available to an employer or to another customer. Each use needs its appropriate information rights, controls and validation.

Specific products and connected-device configurations remain subject to their own development, consent design, testing and acceptance. The October 2026 public company film introduces Biota at division level.
