Purpose, scope and availability+

The compute fabric is an optional architecture for independent background jobs on explicitly enrolled machines. Available capacity, information rights, cancellation and foreground work have to be controlled. Evaluations, document processing and separable research steps are possible test workloads.

Spare capacity is not free computing. Electricity, wear, scheduling, networking and support belong in the complete comparison.
