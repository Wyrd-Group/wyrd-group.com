CORP-US

Local intelligence for protected operations, resilient infrastructure and disconnected environments.

CORP-US is Wyrd's defense, protected-operations, civil-resilience, strategic-infrastructure, robotics, drone, maritime and government division. It runs on the same governed stack as the rest of the group — identity, mandate, command chain, rules of engagement, theater boundary, escalation ladder, dual key, signed proof — deployed where the network is contested and the consequence is physical.

The authority model

Machines that can operate alone, under authority that never leaves the human.

Every CORP-US system inherits the same ICARUS primitives. This is what makes edge autonomy defensible: the node can decide under signal silence because its limits were signed before it left.

What this means operationally

A CORP-US node reasons locally, selects an approved model, acts inside a bounded mandate, refuses what is unauthorized, keeps signed records, and synchronizes when a link returns. Human command is not a UI courtesy — it is a cryptographic precondition. Fail-closed is the default: when authority cannot be verified, the system does not act.

Azrael
The military-operations family. Restricted programme, named and placed.
Restricted
What Wyrd contributes

The governed local brain: edge autonomy that keeps functioning without uplink, a mandate that bounds what the node may do in a defined theater, an escalation ladder that ends in shutdown, and a tamper-evident record of every decision and refusal.

Why it is a separate programme

Because the authority model must be auditable independently of the civilian stack. Azrael carries its own command chain, its own rules of engagement and its own keys.

Release conditions

Lawful customer authority, licensing, export review, procurement rules, independent safety and legal controls, explicit human command and domain-specific approval. Weapons, targeting and offensive-autonomy content is handled in that channel and does not appear in any public or general partner document.

Naming

Azrael is the preferred spelling (recorded variant: Asrael). Never used as a synonym for Samael.
