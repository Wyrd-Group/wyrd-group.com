Purpose, scope and availability+

DAEDALUS is the specialist simulation option for questions that benefit from an explicit domain model. SUM can compare alternatives using permitted institutional state and operational context, with dated assumptions, uncertainty and sensitivity. A simulation cannot authorise or dispatch work.

Adoption begins with a defined scope, the relevant integrations and acceptance in the intended environment. Product and component availability varies by configuration.
