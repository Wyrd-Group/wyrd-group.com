AN OPERATING EXAMPLE

An unusual inverter signal, assessed in context.

OMNIS retrieves permitted operating history and maintenance records. ARGOS connects the inverter to related assets and relevant conditions. SUM coordinates specialist analysis through Cortex and CAOS, including another model’s critique of the proposed diagnosis. WUI presents the evidence and uncertainty to the responsible engineer. Approved follow-up passes through ICARUS and IRIS, and the observed result is retained in the Proof Spine.

Illustrative workflow. Integration and qualification required.
Purpose, scope and availability+

Wyrd Energy brings two connected questions into one technology programme: how to use resources carefully when running AI, and how governed intelligence can help understand and maintain energy infrastructure. WUI provides the shared knowledge, operational context and model-coordination architecture for both.

WUI coordinates intelligence and approved work around energy systems. Responsible engineers and existing certified control systems retain electrical protection and control. A specific telemetry or maintenance integration needs its own technical acceptance.
