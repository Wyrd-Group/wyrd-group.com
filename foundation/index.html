THE FOUNDATION MISSION

Talent is everywhere.
Opportunity should be too.

The Wyrd Foundation is being developed as an independent educational, scientific and talent-development institution. Its global mission is to widen access to learning, research, professional practice and useful projects despite constraints of money, equipment, connectivity or geography. Africa is a principal capacity-building geography within that global mission.

The intended core educational programmes are free to participate in. Local educators and institutions retain authority over their programmes. Learning participation does not create a commercial equity obligation or make learner data a company asset.

INDEPENDENT BY DESIGN

A public mission.
Its own responsibilities.

The Foundation is planned as a legally independent institution with no shareholders, separate from Wyrd Group’s commercial companies. Its governance, accounts, contracts, participant information and programme decisions remain distinct.

Approved Wyrd technology can support education and research through defined zero-cost licences. Educational access gives no authority over commercial customer systems. University nodes retain local curriculum, procurement and data control.

Safeguarding, consent, age-appropriate design, educator support and independent evaluation are requirements of programme delivery. Research needs approved protocols, ethical review and purpose-limited data. Educational participation is voluntary; paid professional work requires a separate agreement.

Foundation income is intended to remain within its educational and scientific mission, supporting access, research, fellowships, safeguarding and programme delivery. The purpose is to expand capability and opportunity while protecting the people taking part.
