Purpose, scope and availability+

Mandate is Wyrd’s proposed interoperable network for governed agentic payments. It links the agent’s principal, delegated economic authority and transaction intent to an authorised financial action. Existing banks, payment providers and rails move the value.

Bounded sandbox payment and paper-trading paths have internal evidence. Production providers, financial reconciliation, institutional participation and the network’s operating framework require their own integration and qualification.
