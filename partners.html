Pilot progression

Eight gates, in order, with a decision at the end.

No stage is skipped to make a demo look better. The last gate is a measured decision — including the option to stop.

01
Diagnostic. The real workflow, the real constraints, and what "better" would mean numerically.
02
Data and authority map. Which data exists, who owns it, who may decide, and what must never be automated.
03
Read-only shadow mode. The system observes and forms opinions; it changes nothing.
04
Offline replay. Historical cases run through the governed path so its verdicts can be compared with what actually happened.
05
Supervised recommendation. Suggestions reach a human, with sources, confidence and the mandate they would need.
06
Bounded approval workflow. A narrow class of actions becomes possible — behind approval, dual key and a receipt.
07
Controlled pilot. One workflow, one bounded scope, measured against the agreed baseline.
08
Measured decision: scale or stop. Recorded either way.
