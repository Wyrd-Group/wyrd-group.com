PRIVACY

This site collects almost nothing. Here is the almost.

A privacy notice for a company whose product is data restraint should be short. This one is.

01
No analytics, no trackers, no advertising, no cookies set by us. The site is static files. We do not profile visitors and there is nothing to consent to, so there is no cookie banner.
02
Hosting logs. Our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) for security and operations, retained briefly per their policy. We do not enrich or join them with anything.
03
Fonts. The site loads DM Sans from Google Fonts. Your browser requests font files from Google, which receives your IP address. The stock photographs and background videos are served from this website.
04
Contact links open your own mail client. These links compose a message that you review and send yourself. The website does not collect the message. What you send us is used to evaluate and reply to your enquiry, kept confidential, and never sold or shared beyond that purpose.
06
Your rights. Under GDPR you can request access, correction or deletion of anything you have sent us: alec.carvalho@wyrd-group.com. You may also complain to your supervisory authority; ours is the CNPD (Portugal).
Website operator and privacy contact

Alec de Carvalho operates this website under the Wyrd Group name. For privacy enquiries, contact alec.carvalho@wyrd-group.com. This notice was updated on 4 October 2026.
