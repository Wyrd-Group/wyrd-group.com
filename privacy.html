Privacy

This site collects almost nothing. Here is the almost.

A privacy notice for a company whose product is data restraint should be short. This one is.

01
No analytics, no trackers, no advertising, no cookies set by us. The site is static files. We do not profile visitors and there is nothing to consent to, so there is no cookie banner.
02
Hosting logs. Our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) for security and operations, retained briefly per their policy. We do not enrich or join them with anything.
03
Fonts. Pages currently load two typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests files from Google and Google sees your IP address. We plan to self-host. Until then, this is the one third-party request on the Wyrd site.
04
The partner form sends an email — from your own mail client. Nothing is stored or transmitted by this website; the form composes a message you review and send yourself. What you send us is used to evaluate and reply to your enquiry, kept confidential, and never sold or shared beyond that purpose.
05
Ask Wyrd runs in your browser. Questions typed there are matched against a knowledge base on the page itself. They are not transmitted to us.
06
Your rights. Under GDPR you can request access, correction or deletion of anything you have sent us: privacy@wyrd-group.com. You may also complain to your supervisory authority; ours is the CNPD (Portugal).
