Why there is no single number

We do not add test counts together. Doing so would require duplicate tests removed, branches pinned, dates current, commands reproducible, skips disclosed, and generated checks separated from behavioural tests. Until all six hold for a combined figure, individual cards are the honest form.

And software test evidence proves engineering work. It does not automatically prove safety, field reliability, clinical performance, regulatory approval, customer value or production security.
