Purpose, scope and availability+

RASOS supports governed deployment, updates, runtime assurance, drift handling, repair and rollback. The accepted configuration and its lifecycle rules stay explicit. A target-specific qualification determines how release changes and recovery are admitted.

Adoption begins with a defined scope, the relevant integrations and acceptance in the intended environment. Product and component availability varies by configuration.
