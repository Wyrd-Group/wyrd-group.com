Purpose, scope and availability+

REFLEX is designed around narrow, typed decisions and a two-level evaluation path. A small first tier handles eligible cases, while uncertainty or defined conditions send a case to a more capable review tier.

A model’s confidence and a fast answer do not establish authority. REFLEX supplies a decision or recommendation within a defined task; ICARUS checks whether an actual consequential action is permitted.
