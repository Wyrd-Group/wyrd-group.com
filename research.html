Research

Wyrd Labs gives unconventional questions a serious engineering trial.

The divisions commercialize only the ideas that survive evidence. Everything on this page is labelled by what it currently is — an active program, a prototype, a hypothesis under test, or a recorded option.

The method

Innocence Translation.

It does not mean turning dangerous technology into a civilian product. It is a discovery method that protects curiosity without protecting weak ideas from evidence.

01
Put an intelligent person who has not been conditioned by the industry's assumptions in front of the problem.
02
Let that person ask questions experts may consider naive, impossible or already settled.
03
Translate the question into technical primitives.
04
Ask domain experts to attack it.
05
Build a cheap model, simulation, benchmark or prototype.
06
Compare it against strong baselines.
07
Check law, ethics, cost, safety and deployment reality.
08
Keep it only if the evidence survives.

Innocence asks the question. Expertise tries to kill it. Proof decides.

Two things this page is not

It is not a claim that any of these exist as products. And where a recorded idea has been parked or rejected — a from-scratch OS kernel, a phone manufactured as version one, a weaponized RADOME, an agent currency before authorization — it stays parked. The option portfolio demonstrates reach. It does not replace focus.

Restraint

Conflict as a quality mechanism.

An idea is not protected because it is ours. It is attacked, repaired, retested — and then it survives or it dies.

idea→ attack→ repair→ retest→ survive or die
