Models, hardware, calibration and limitations

Apple M1 Pro with 16 GB unified memory, local MLX. The first tier is Wyrd's fine-tuned encoder. The frozen Qwen3.5-4B reviewer uses 4-bit weights and direct-logit scoring. Gate thresholds were fitted on a separate synthetic calibration holdout before the public run.

The reviewer median was 1.094 seconds on its 89 actual public escalations. That subset is different from all 231 items, so it is not a matched speed comparison with the 20.94 ms first-tier median. No complete reviewer-only public run establishes a cascade advantage over that model alone.

Calibration remains weak. First-tier accepted items were 53.5% correct, and no yes/no items were escalated in this run. The public sample is small. This is a local research result, not an official leaderboard score or a customer performance promise.

Provenance and what was verified

Governance, timing, real-model and WUI-continuity figures come from Wyrd's retained 2 October governed-execution campaign and its public evidence export. Scenario and run totals were checked against aggregate CSVs and the 30,000 primary attempt rows. The private full replay verified 84,300 signed native records. Public verification covers primary relationships and native creation bindings; secondary and race classifications include publisher observations.

REFLEX figures were checked against the complete 23 September public-item report, aggregate JSON and saved prediction rows. Percentages use correct items divided by attempted items; no group-average percentages or average percentiles are substituted for totals.

Mandate figures come from the retained 4 October merged-package receipt and separate installed demonstrations. Native ICARUS candidate checks were recorded later on 4 October and are unsigned. Neither later result is presented as a rerun of the frozen 2 October campaign.

All of this is internal evidence. Independent custody and external assurance remain open. Cryptographic trace integrity does not establish source truth, model accuracy, immunity to every attack or a population-wide failure probability.
