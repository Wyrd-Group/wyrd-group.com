Purpose, scope and availability+

SUM is WUI’s executive intelligence. It understands configured objectives, advises users, makes decisions within permitted authority, explores possible future pathways and risks, and manages, coordinates, orchestrates and monitors work live. Cortex and CAOS coordinate the analytical workforce; SUM synthesises its results against objectives and constraints. IRIS carries approved assignments to accountable endpoints.

Predictions and scenarios express possible futures and their uncertainty. SUM’s reasoning does not itself grant authority. ICARUS checks consequential actions, and responsible users retain decisions and approvals required by the configured policy. Cogito ergo SUM describes the product’s executive role.
