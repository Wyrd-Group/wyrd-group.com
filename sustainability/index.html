Purpose, scope and availability+

Wyrd’s sustainability approach is to avoid unnecessary large-model work, use suitable existing equipment and build maintainable local capability. Local inference is not inherently more efficient. Routing, idle power, retries, shared services, cooling and hardware must enter the same comparison.

No deployment-wide energy saving is claimed before a matched trial. Renewable supply and location-specific impacts are measured separately from software efficiency.
