Purpose, scope and availability+

TrueSight supplies evidence-checking and reconstruction capabilities. It separates a reported result from the material needed to verify it. A verifier’s scope, trust anchors and administrative custody must be understood in each installation.

Adoption begins with a defined scope, the relevant integrations and acceptance in the intended environment. Product and component availability varies by configuration.
