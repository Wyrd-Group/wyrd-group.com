A bank employee responding to a service disruption.

The employee retrieves the approved procedure, prepares an explanation for customers and sends the draft for review. A consequential send or system change must follow the relevant authority check. WUI for Work can connect this local task to the institution-wide response.

Purpose, scope and availability+

Wyrd Work brings documents, WyrdMail, meetings through Rooms, Studio and the ALEC personal assistant into a common working environment. Routine AI assistance belongs to the base product: drafting, summarising, retrieving authorised information and helping with everyday work.

Work can be adopted independently. Its included AI does not require purchasing WUI for Work. Connecting the two products joins the employee’s workspace to a wider institutional operating picture.
