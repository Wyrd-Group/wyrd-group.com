Purpose, scope and availability+

WUI for Work is the first dedicated institutional workplace configuration of Wyrd Unified Intelligence, offered alongside the complete WUI platform. Its models and agents are tailored to the institution’s approved material, terminology, workflows and controls. It connects the institution’s permitted knowledge, live operational activity and relevant external developments. It coordinates analysis across suitable models, helps management assess situations against approved priorities and connects decisions to accountable assignments and verifiable outcomes.

It can connect to Wyrd Work or to existing systems through agreed integrations. Work includes its own routine AI. WUI for Work adds the shared institutional operating picture and coordination layer.
