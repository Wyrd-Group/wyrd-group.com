Purpose, scope and availability+

Wyrd offers WUI as a complete, configured intelligence and operations package. People, institutions, cities, infrastructure, digital systems, machines, fleets and combinations of these are possible client environments. OMNIS supplies permitted knowledge and memory. ARGOS technology supplies perception and ontology. SUM reasons about objectives, advises users, explores future pathways and risks, makes permitted decisions and manages, coordinates, orchestrates and monitors work continuously. Agentix supplies connected digital reach; Corpus machines can provide separately qualified physical reach. We select the modules, interfaces, models and agents around the client’s objectives, terminology, workflows and operating constraints. Models and agents can be tailored, trained where appropriate and evaluated using expressly approved material.

Wyrd is the broader ecosystem. Wyrd Unified Intelligence is both the modular platform and its configured commercial package. Our first commercial platform offers are WUI and WUI for Work, its dedicated institutional workplace configuration. Wyrd Work remains a separate employee product with its own included AI. The ARGOS department is broader than its technology inside WUI; Corpus remains a division whose qualified systems can connect to WUI. Each configuration defines its information access, capabilities, authority, infrastructure and acceptance requirements.
